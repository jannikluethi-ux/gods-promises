His Promise Daily

Privacy

His Promise Daily is run by Jannik Luethi. Questions about privacy: Email Jannik Luethi (jannik.luethi@proton.me).

Accounts

If you create an account, His Promise Daily stores your email address, optional display name, the Bible version you choose while signed in, and the promises you mark as favorites with our database provider, Supabase (project region Zurich). Your password is handled by Supabase Auth. We do not write it into the site’s own database or into browser storage. A sign-in session is kept in this browser by the Supabase client so you can stay signed in; it is not a password. Favorites and account settings are available on another device after you sign in there. Older profiles that existed only in a browser are not copied over.

You can delete the account from the account page. That removes the sign-in, the profile, and the favorites. Explanation feedback already sent is kept, without your name. The chosen Bible version on a signed-out browser, and verse text from other translations, may still be cached on that device until you clear site data. If you email us, we receive your email address and whatever you write.

Morning promise email

If you are signed in, you can turn on “Email me one promise each morning.” That choice is stored on your profile and is off until you turn it on. Each morning the site then emails that day’s verse, its short explanation, and a link to the verse page, to the email address on your account. The message comes from His Promise Daily at no-reply@hispromisedaily.com and is delivered by Resend. You can turn it off on the account page, or with the unsubscribe link in the message. That link does not ask you to sign in. We do not sell the address, and we do not send this email to anyone who has not turned it on.

Feedback on explanations

Under each explanation you can optionally answer “Was this explanation helpful?”. You can send this without an account. If you send feedback, we store your answer, any reasons you tick, any comment you write, which verse and which version of the explanation it was about, and the time. If you are signed in, the server checks your session and may store your account id with that feedback. It does not store your email address in the feedback row. Please don’t include personal details in comments.

To prevent duplicate and automated submissions, sending feedback sets a cookie (hpd_fb) containing a random ID that is only sent back to our feedback service; we store it only in hashed form. A hashed form of your IP address is kept briefly to limit abuse. Feedback is stored with our database provider, Supabase, and is used only to review and improve the explanations.

Services your browser contacts

These providers handle data under their own policies, which we do not control.

  • Hosting – Vercel (hispromisedaily.com): the main address is served by Vercel Inc. Like any web host, Vercel receives technical request data such as your IP address, and location derived from it, when your browser loads the site. Vercel Privacy Policy
  • Hosting – GitHub Pages (old address, jannikluethi-ux.github.io): GitHub states that when a GitHub Pages site is visited, the visitor’s IP address is logged and stored for security purposes. GitHub Pages data collection · GitHub Privacy Statement
  • Accounts – Supabase: sign-in, profiles, and favorites are stored by Supabase (hosted in Zurich for this project). Supabase receives your email address and the account data described above when you register or sign in. Supabase Privacy Policy
  • Fonts – Google Fonts: your browser requests fonts from fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com. Google states these requests include your IP address, the requested URL and HTTP headers such as your browser’s user agent and the referring page, that the Fonts API does not set or log cookies, and that this data is not used to build profiles or for targeted advertising. Google Fonts privacy FAQ
  • Bible versions – bible-api.com: if you choose ASV, Young's Literal, Darby, or Douay-Rheims, your browser requests that verse text from bible-api.com (run by Tim Morgan). King James text, only after this site has explicitly turned that option on for the country, is loaded from this site, not from that service. The request contains the verse reference and translation, and like any web request it reveals your IP address and may include our site address as the referrer. Your search words are not part of these requests. The service says it rate-limits by IP address; we could not find a privacy policy for it. bible-api.com
  • Support payments – Stripe: the “Support in USD” link opens Stripe’s hosted checkout. Nothing goes to Stripe unless you follow that link. Stripe’s policy says it may collect information entered into a checkout form even if you don’t complete it, and that it may transfer personal data to other countries, including the United States and India. Stripe Privacy Policy
  • Morning email – Resend: only if you turn on the morning promise, Resend receives your email address, the name on your profile if you set one, and the verse we ask it to send. Resend Privacy Policy

Visitor statistics

On hispromisedaily.com only, the site uses Vercel Web Analytics to count page views and estimate how many visitors it has. These are anonymous estimates, not counts of confirmed people. It is not loaded on the old GitHub address or on preview addresses.

Before anything is sent, the site removes every query string and “#” fragment from the address and only reports its own known pages. Search words, sign-in redirect values, email addresses, profiles and saved verses are never sent, and no custom events are recorded.

Vercel states that Web Analytics uses no third-party cookies; visitors are recognised by a hash created from the incoming request, which is discarded after 24 hours. Each page view may be stored with a timestamp, the page address, the referring site, location (country, region, city), operating system and browser with versions, device type and the script version. Vercel’s privacy notice also says it collects information about traffic to its customers’ websites, such as visitor IP address and location derived from it. Vercel Web Analytics privacy · Vercel Privacy Notice

Cookies and other tracking

This site’s own code sets no cookies except the feedback cookie described above, which is set only if you send explanation feedback. The sign-in session is kept in this browser’s storage by the Supabase client, not in a cookie written by this site. The site includes no advertising, session-replay or other tracking scripts. The third-party services above may process data as described in their own policies.

If you add His Promise Daily to your home screen, this browser may keep a copy of the app and the current daily verse on your device so you can open it offline. That copy stays on the device. Sign-in, explanation feedback, and other requests to our servers are not answered from that copy.
