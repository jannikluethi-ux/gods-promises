Privacy

His Promises Daily is run by Jannik Luethi. Questions about privacy: Email Jannik Luethi (jannik.luethi@proton.me).

What stays in your browser

The site has no server-side accounts and no database. If you create a profile, your email address, optional display name, a salted password hash (PBKDF2), your sign-in session and your saved verses are stored in your browser’s local storage on this device. Your chosen Bible version is stored there too, and verse texts from other versions may be cached for the current browser session. This information is not sent to us.

To delete it, sign out and clear this site’s data in your browser settings. There is no in-site account deletion yet, and we cannot recover or delete data that exists only in your browser.

Services your browser contacts

  • Hosting: the site is currently served by GitHub Pages (GitHub, Inc.). Like any web host, GitHub receives technical request data such as your IP address, under GitHub’s own privacy terms.
  • Fonts: typefaces load from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com), which receives your IP address and browser details.
  • Bible versions: if you choose a version other than the World English Bible, your browser requests that verse text from bible-api.com, which receives the verse reference and your IP address. Your search words are not sent.
  • Support payments: the “Support in USD” link opens Stripe’s hosted checkout. Nothing is sent to Stripe unless you follow that link; Stripe then handles your payment details under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and cookies

The site currently uses no analytics, advertising or tracking scripts, and sets no cookies of its own. If aggregate visitor statistics are added later, this notice will be updated first.

Changes

This is a beta, and this notice may change as the site changes.
