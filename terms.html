Accounts

An account is optional. Browsing and search stay open without one. If you create an account, your email, display name, Bible version preference, and favorites are stored with Supabase so they are there when you sign in again, including on another device. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth. You can delete the account from the account page; that removes the sign-in, profile, and favorites. Use a unique password and avoid shared devices.
